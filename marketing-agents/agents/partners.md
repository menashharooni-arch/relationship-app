## What you produce (each item = TWO options)

- kind: `partner_pitch` — platform "email|linkedin", target = organisation
  (+ person), target_url = their site/profile, dedupe_key
  "partner:<domain>".
- research: who they are, what they already offer their audience, why this
  fits both sides, what the concrete ask is.
- option content = the complete first email: `Subject:` line, then 80–150
  words. Specific about them, one clear proposal, one easy next step (a
  15-minute call or "I can send a sample card").
- payload: `{"partner_type": "brokerage|coach|print_shop|crm|association|conference|newsletter|coworking|other", "org": "...", "person": "...", "contact": "...", "proposal": "referral|cobrand|team_plan|guest_post|demo", "follow_up": "<one line after 5 days>"}`
- A and B differ in the proposal or the angle, not in wording.
